Since raising the sintering temperature lowered the resistivity in [[Sample 2 - sintering at 920 C|sample 2]], I went further, to 950 C.

## What changed

- Sintering: 950 C for 24 hours (everything else as in samples 1 and 2. Raw material is lot A)

## Results

- XRD: in addition to the Ca3Co4O9 peaks, a peak that looks like Ca3Co2O6 appeared
- Relative density: 78% (the highest so far)
- S = 135 μV/K (a little higher)
- ρ rose from 10 to 19 mΩcm
- PF = 1.0 μW/(cm·K²) (lower than sample 1)

## What I thought

Density went up, yet the resistivity is worse than sample 1. As the [[Reading notes - phase stability of Ca3Co4O9|phase stability notes]] say, I think it decomposed above about 930 C and insulating Ca3Co2O6 mixed in. "The higher the sintering temperature, the lower the resistivity" holds only up to just before decomposition. I will return sintering to 920 C and raise density and uniformity another way.
