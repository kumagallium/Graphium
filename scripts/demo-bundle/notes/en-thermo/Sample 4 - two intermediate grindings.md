Sintering goes back to the 920 C of [[Sample 2 - sintering at 920 C|sample 2]], and I added one grinding and one recalcination after calcination. The aim is to make the reaction uniform.

## What changed

- Calcination 900 C for 12 hours, grind 15 minutes, 900 C for 12 hours again, grind 15 minutes (2 intermediate grindings)
- Sintering at 920 C for 24 hours. Raw material is lot A

## Results

- XRD: only Ca3Co4O9 peaks. The peaks are a little narrower than in sample 2
- Relative density: 73%
- S = 127 μV/K
- ρ dropped from 10 to 8.5 mΩcm
- PF = 1.9 μW/(cm·K²)

## What I thought

I lowered the resistivity without raising the temperature. I think grinding and remixing in between reduced the unreacted parts. Of everything so far, these conditions are the easiest to work with. I will use them as the baseline from now on.
