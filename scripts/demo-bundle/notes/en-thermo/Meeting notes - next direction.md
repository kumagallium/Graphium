Notes from the lab meeting, where I showed the 7 samples so far and asked for advice.

## What we discussed

- Cap the sintering temperature at 920 C. Combining the decomposition in [[Sample 3 - sintering at 950 C|sample 3]] with the +11 C offset in the [[Equipment notes - furnace temperature calibration|furnace calibration]], the setting should not go above 930 C
- The failure of [[Sample 7 - repeat with a new lot of CaCO3|sample 7]] to reproduce: add drying of the raw material to the procedure, then remake it. Weigh before and after drying and record how much water there was
- The Bi substitution in [[Sample 6 - partial replacement of Ca with Bi|sample 6]] looks promising. But vary it only after the baseline reproduces

## Decisions

1. Dry CaCO3 at 120 C for 4 hours, cool it in a desiccator, then weigh
2. Remake the sample 4 conditions with the dried lot B
3. Once it reproduces, vary the Bi substitution at 0, 2.5, 5, 7.5 and 10%
