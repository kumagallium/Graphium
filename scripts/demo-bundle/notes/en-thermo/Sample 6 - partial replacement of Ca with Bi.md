Under the conditions of [[Sample 4 - two intermediate grindings|sample 4]], I replaced 5% of the Ca with Bi. In Ca3Co4O9, Bi substitution can raise S.

## What changed

- Composition: (Ca0.95Bi0.05)3Co4O9. Weighed with Bi2O3 added
- Everything else as in sample 4 (2 intermediate grindings, sintering at 920 C, pressing at 100 MPa. Raw material is lot A)

## Results

- XRD: only Ca3Co4O9 peaks. The peak positions shifted slightly toward lower angles
- Relative density: 74%
- S rose from 127 to 141 μV/K
- ρ = 9.2 mΩcm
- PF = 2.2 μW/(cm·K²) (the highest so far)

## What I thought

ρ barely changed and only S went up. Varying the amount of substitution may improve it further. Next I vary it from 0 to 10%.
