Kept the conditions of [[Sample 1 - baseline (sintering at 900 C)|sample 1]] and changed only the sintering temperature, from 900 C to 920 C.

## What changed

- Sintering: 920 C for 24 hours (everything else as in sample 1. Raw material is lot A)

## Results

- XRD: only Ca3Co4O9 peaks. Still single phase
- Relative density: 65% to 72%
- S = 126 μV/K (about the same as sample 1)
- ρ dropped from 14 to 10 mΩcm
- PF = 1.6 μW/(cm·K²)

## What I thought

S barely changed and only ρ dropped. I think the density rose and the grains connected better. But in the [[Equipment notes - furnace temperature calibration|furnace calibration]], a setpoint of 920 C gives an actual 931 C, close to where decomposition starts. In [[Sample 3 - sintering at 950 C|sample 3]], I see what happens when I go higher still.
