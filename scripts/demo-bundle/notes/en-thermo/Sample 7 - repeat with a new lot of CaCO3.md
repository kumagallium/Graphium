I remade the [[Sample 4 - two intermediate grindings|sample 4]] conditions with only the raw material CaCO3 changed to a new lot (lot B). A check on whether the baseline conditions reproduce.

## Conditions

- Raw materials: CaCO3 (lot B, just opened) and Co3O4. Weighed at Ca : Co = 3 : 4
- Everything else as in sample 4 (2 intermediate grindings, sintering at 920 C, pressing at 100 MPa)
- It rained on the day of weighing, and the humidity in the lab was high

## Results

- XRD: in addition to the Ca3Co4O9 peaks, a small peak that looks like Co3O4 remained
- Relative density: 71%
- S = 124 μV/K
- ρ rose from 8.5 to 13 mΩcm
- Under the same conditions as sample 4, the result went back to about the level of sample 1

## What I thought

The sintering temperature is the same 920 C as sample 4, so this is not decomposition like [[Sample 3 - sintering at 950 C|sample 3]]. What remained is Co3O4, so the composition has shifted toward too little Ca. I think the new lot of CaCO3 had absorbed moisture, and part of the weighed mass was water. The conditions are not set by temperature and time alone; the state of the raw material has to be part of them. Next I dry the CaCO3 at 120 C before weighing.
