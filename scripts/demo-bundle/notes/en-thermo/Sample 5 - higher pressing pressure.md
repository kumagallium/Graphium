Under the conditions of [[Sample 4 - two intermediate grindings|sample 4]], I raised only the pellet pressing pressure, from 100 MPa to 200 MPa. I want a bit more density.

## What changed

- Pressing: 200 MPa (everything else as in sample 4. Raw material is lot A)

## Results

- After sintering, 2 thin cracks formed at the edge of the pellet. I cut the measurement bar from an uncracked part
- XRD: only Ca3Co4O9 peaks
- Relative density: 76%
- S = 128 μV/K
- ρ = 9.0 mΩcm (about the same as sample 4)

## What I thought

Density went up, but the cracks made it hard to handle and ρ did not improve. I will not raise the pressing pressure any further and will go back to 100 MPa.
