The baseline sample of Ca3Co4O9. Made first under textbook conditions, then I move one condition at a time from here.

## Conditions

- Raw materials: CaCO3 (lot A) and Co3O4, weighed at Ca : Co = 3 : 4
- Mixing: 30 minutes in an agate mortar (wet mixing with a little ethanol)
- Calcination: 900 C for 12 hours in air
- Grinding and pressing: ground 15 minutes in a mortar, then pressed into a 13 mm diameter pellet at 100 MPa
- Sintering: 900 C for 24 hours in air
- Measured as in the [[Reading notes - measuring the Seebeck coefficient and its errors|measurement notes]]

## Results

- XRD: only Ca3Co4O9 peaks. No impurity peaks visible
- Relative density: 65%
- Room-temperature Seebeck coefficient S = 128 μV/K (±2 μV/K over 3 measurements)
- Electrical resistivity ρ = 14 mΩcm
- Power factor PF = 1.2 μW/(cm·K²)
