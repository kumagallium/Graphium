The crust color kept coming out paler than the books describe, so I measured what the oven actually does.

## Method

- Oven thermometer on the middle rack, center
- Dial set to 200, 230 and 250 °C; after 20 minutes of preheating, read the thermometer
- Two runs each

## Result

| Dial | Thermometer (run 1) | Thermometer (run 2) |
|---|---|---|
| 200 °C | 186 °C | 184 °C |
| 230 °C | 214 °C | 216 °C |
| 250 °C | 234 °C | 236 °C |

- Every setting runs 14–16 °C below the dial. The offset is nearly constant
- Opening the door drops it by almost 30 °C, and it takes 5 minutes to recover

## Decisions

- When a recipe says 230 °C, set the dial to 245 °C
- Preheat at least 20 minutes before loading. Keep the door open as briefly as possible
- The "230 °C" in trials 1–7 was really about 215 °C. That explains the pale crust
