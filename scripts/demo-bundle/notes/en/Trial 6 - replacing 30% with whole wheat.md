Trial 5 process (12 hours cold), with 30% of the flour replaced by whole wheat.

## Formula

- Bread flour (12.5%) 210 g + whole-wheat flour 90 g
- Water 225 g (75% hydration)
- Sourdough starter 60 g, salt 6 g

## During the process

- Stiff right after mixing, clearly short of water compared with trial 5. The bran seems to soak it up
- During folds the dough tore instead of stretching

## Result

- Height 9 cm. Tight crumb with small holes. Nothing like trial 5
- More nutty aroma. Thicker crust
- Same 75% hydration, and changing the flour alone did this

## Lesson and next

With whole wheat the hydration probably needs to go up to about 80%. But before that, I want to check whether "more water, more open crumb" really holds regardless of the flour.
