A baseline loaf. From here I will change one thing at a time.

## Formula

- Bread flour (12.5% protein) 300 g
- Water 195 g (65% hydration)
- Sourdough starter 60 g (20% of flour)
- Salt 6 g

## Process

1. Mix flour and water, rest 30 minutes (autolyse)
2. Add starter and salt, knead 10 minutes
3. Bulk fermentation: 4 hours at 26 °C, two folds along the way
4. Shape, into the tin, proof 2 hours at 26 °C
5. Bake 35 minutes at 230 °C (dial setting)

## Result

- Height 9.5 cm. Crumb fine and even, a little tight
- Moist but heavy. Toasts with a good bite
- The dough was cohesive and easy to handle
