Building a sourdough starter from flour and water only, without commercial yeast.

## Mix and routine

- Day 1: 50 g whole-wheat flour + 50 g water (30 °C), lid loosely on, kept on a shelf at 26 °C
- From day 2: keep 50 g of starter, feed 50 g whole-wheat flour + 50 g water every day (discard goes to the fridge)
- Day 3: sour smell, tiny bubbles
- Day 5: doubled in volume 6 hours after feeding
- Day 7: doubles in 4–5 hours. The peak time has settled, so I will start baking with it

## Notes

- On a day when the room dropped to 22 °C it took 8 hours to double. Starter activity depends a lot on temperature
- Water at 30 °C gets it going faster. Cold tap water set it back by half a day
- On day 7 the starter peaks about 4 hours after feeding. Past the peak it sinks and turns sharply sour
