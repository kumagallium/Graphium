Same formula as [[Trial 1 - basic loaf at 65% hydration|trial 1]], water raised from 195 g to 210 g (70% hydration).

## What changed

- Water 210 g (70% hydration). Everything else as in trial 1 (bread flour 12.5%, starter 20%, salt 2%, 26 °C)

## During the process

- Sticky at the start, but the dough came together after 10 minutes of kneading
- Bulk fermentation reached about 1.8× volume in 4 hours (trial 1 was about 1.6×)

## Result

- Height 10.5 cm. The holes are clearly larger than trial 1, somewhat uneven here and there
- Lighter texture. Thinner crust
- Surprising how much a 5% difference in hydration changes
