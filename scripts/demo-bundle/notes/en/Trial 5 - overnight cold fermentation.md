After the over-fermentation of trial 4, the opposite: cold and long. Trial 3 formula (75% hydration, bread flour 12.5%).

## What changed

- Bulk: 2 hours at 26 °C (three folds), then 12 hours in the fridge at 4 °C
- Next morning, shaped straight from the fridge, 2 hours final proof at 26 °C

## During the process

- The cold dough was easy to handle and shaped cleanly
- Final proof started slowly but reached the rim of the tin in 2 hours

## Result

- Height 11 cm. Crumb as open as trial 3, and the holes are more even in size
- The flavor is clearly different: wheat sweetness, mild acidity, deeper aroma
- Still moist the next day

## Summary

Cold, long fermentation gave the same rise as trial 3 with better flavor and aroma. It also fits a weekday rhythm: mix in the evening, bake in the morning.
