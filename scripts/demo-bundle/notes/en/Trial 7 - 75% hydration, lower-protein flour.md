Testing the conclusion from trial 3 ("more water, more open crumb") with a different flour. Hydration kept at 75%, flour switched to an all-purpose type with 11.0% protein.

## Formula

- All-purpose flour (11.0% protein) 300 g
- Water 225 g (75% hydration)
- Sourdough starter 60 g, salt 6 g
- Process as in trial 3 (26 °C, 4 hours, four sets of folds)

## During the process

- Even after four sets of folds the dough never tightened. It stayed sticky and never turned glossy
- Out of the bowl after bulk, the dough spread sideways and could not hold a shape

## Result

- Height 7.5 cm. Spread out and flat. Tight crumb with no large holes. The bottom is dense
- Chewy but heavy
- Same 75% hydration as trial 3, opposite result

## Thinking it through

The crumb opened at 75% because the bread flour (12.5%) could hold that much water. With 11% protein there was too much water for the gluten network. "More water, more open crumb" holds only while the flour has enough protein. When changing flour, the hydration has to be chosen again for that flour.
