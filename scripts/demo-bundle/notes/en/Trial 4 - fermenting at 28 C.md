Trial 3 formula (75% hydration, bread flour 12.5%), fermentation temperature raised from 26 °C to 28 °C. Same 4 hours.

## What changed

- Bulk fermentation 4 hours at 28 °C (used the oven's proofing mode)
- Final proof also at 28 °C, 2 hours

## During the process

- Volume had already passed 2× after 3 hours. At 4 hours it was overblown, with big bubbles at the surface
- The dough had little tension when shaping and slumped

## Result

- The middle sank during baking, height 8 cm. Crumb uneven: big caverns next to dense patches
- Strongly sour. Crust stayed pale
- Over-fermented, I think. At 28 °C, 4 hours was too long

## Lesson

A 2 °C rise changed the fermentation speed a lot. Raising the temperature means shortening the time.
