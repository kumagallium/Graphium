Hydration up again to 75% (water 225 g). Same bread flour as trials 1 and 2 (12.5% protein).

## What changed

- Water 225 g (75% hydration)
- Kneading by hand was impossible, so I switched to stretch-and-fold in the bowl: four sets, 30 minutes apart

## During the process

- Almost liquid at first. After the third set of folds the dough tightened up and turned glossy
- Bulk fermentation passed 2× volume in 4 hours

## Result

- Height 11 cm. Large holes, a beautifully open crumb. The cell walls are thin enough to see light through
- Deep color, crisp crust
- Best so far. "More water, more open crumb" seems to be the right reading
