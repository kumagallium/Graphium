Sourdough sandwich loaves, changing one condition at a time. Fix a baseline first, then move hydration → fermentation temperature → fermentation time → flour, in that order. This is the index; the details are in each trial's record.

## The trials in order

1. [[Trial 1 - basic loaf at 65% hydration|Trial 1]] — baseline. 65% hydration, bread flour. Fine, tight crumb
2. [[Trial 2 - 70% hydration|Trial 2]] — 70% hydration. Larger holes, lighter loaf
3. [[Trial 3 - 75% hydration, bread flour|Trial 3]] — 75% hydration. The most open crumb so far
4. [[Trial 4 - fermenting at 28 C|Trial 4]] — 28 °C for 4 hours. Over-fermented, the middle sank
5. [[Trial 5 - overnight cold fermentation|Trial 5]] — 12 hours cold. Same rise as trial 3, better flavor
6. [[Trial 6 - replacing 30% with whole wheat|Trial 6]] — 30% whole wheat. Short of water, tight crumb
7. [[Trial 7 - 75% hydration, lower-protein flour|Trial 7]] — all-purpose flour at 75%. Spread out and flat

## What I know so far

- More water opens the crumb, but only while the flour has enough protein (trial 3 against trial 7)
- Raise the fermentation temperature and the time must come down. Cold and long improves flavor
- When changing flour, choose the hydration again for that flour
